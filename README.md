# PDF Maker

A simple Android app for making PDFs from images.(made with the help of ai studios , and lots of other ai bots, lots of them)

## Why?

Many PDF apps make a simple task complicated with ads, filters,
autocropping, and lots of unnecessary options.

This app was made for older and non-tech-savvy people who just
want to select their photos and make a PDF. That's exactly what this app does just go to your familiar
gallery select photos click share select pdf_maker app and hit generate pdf. 

> Making PDFs as easy as sharing photos on WhatsApp.
## Features
- YOu CAN SAHRE IMAGES FROM GALLERY TO PDF_maker app it opens a preview screem
- heree you can crop, rotate, move images up or down or merge them and there is toggle to hide this buttons in settings if you want
- Simple, minimal interface, easy sharing and renaming and saving no confusion.
- Designed with accessibility and ease of use in mind
- No ads
- works offline
